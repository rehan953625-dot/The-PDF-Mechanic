Frequently Asked Questions

How does SKU sorting speed up packing?

It groups identical products together in the print sequence. Your packing assistant picks 50 units of the same SKU from warehouse racks in one trip.

What printer settings should I use?

Set paper size to 4×6 inches (100×150 mm) and scale to 100% (Actual Size). Avoid "Fit to Page" to prevent barcode ratio distortion.

High-Speed Meesho Shipping Label Processing for Indian E-Commerce Suppliers

Meesho order PDFs package both the courier shipping label and the customer Tax Invoice onto one single A4 page. Feeding this file directly to a 4x6 inch thermal roll printer causes the driver to shrink the entire page, reducing barcode thickness and resulting in courier pickup rejections.

The PDF Mechanic Meesho Tool isolates the top label portion at high vector resolution, groups matching SKUs for swift warehouse picking, organizes Delhivery, Valmo, Shadowfax, and Xpressbees batches, and generates separate invoice and picklist PDFs—all 100% offline inside your browser.
