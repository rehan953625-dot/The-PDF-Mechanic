Hardware Calibration Guide

Recommended Thermal Printer Settings for Meesho Suppliers

To prevent barcode scanning issues during Valmo, Delhivery, or Shadowfax pickups and ensure dark, sharp prints on 203 DPI & 300 DPI thermal heads:

Paper / Media Stock Size Set Page Size to 4.00 × 6.00 inches (or 100 × 150 mm). Never leave stock set to Letter or A4 in printer preferences.
Print Scaling / Size Select Actual Size (100%). Disable "Fit to Printable Area" or "Shrink Oversized Pages" to preserve barcode aspect ratios.
Darkness / Density Set darkness to 12 - 14 (on TSC TE244 / TVS LP 46 / Zebra ZD230) to produce bold black tracking barcodes without bleeding.
Print Speed Set print speed to 3 or 4 inches per second (ips) for the cleanest bar definition on 1D barcodes and QR codes.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Common questions answered about Meesho label cropping, invoice separation, SKU sorting, and thermal roll printing.

How do I crop Meesho shipping labels for a 4x6 thermal printer?

Upload your Meesho order PDF to The PDF Mechanic, select the 'Thermal 4x6' option, choose SKU sorting, and click 'Crop & Prepare Labels'. The tool extracts the label section at 100x150 mm resolution for instant printing.

Why does my Meesho label print tiny or cut off?

Meesho exports labels on full A4 sheets alongside the tax invoice. When printing directly to a 4x6 thermal printer, the driver shrinks the entire A4 sheet into a tiny box. Cropping the label to true 4x6 size solves this instantly.

Does this tool support Valmo, Delhivery, Shadowfax, and Xpressbees?

Yes. Our tool reads the PDF text stream to detect logistics partners (Valmo, Delhivery, Shadowfax, Xpressbees) and groups orders accordingly for smooth courier pickup handovers.

How does SKU sorting speed up warehouse packing?

It organizes matching products sequentially in the print batch. Packaging staff can pick 50 units of the same item from inventory shelves in one trip, pack all 50 identical parcels, and eliminate wrong-item dispatch errors.

Is my customer data and GST billing information safe?

Yes, 100%. The PDF Mechanic processes all PDF rendering, boundary detection, and cropping locally inside your browser memory using client-side JavaScript. No customer addresses or financial data are ever transmitted to any server.

Can I print Meesho shipping labels on regular A4 printers?

Yes. Select the "A4 4-in-1" option to fit four cropped shipping labels onto a single A4 sheet with dashed cut lines, saving 75% on paper and sticker sheet costs.

What is the Multi-Qty Warning Badge?

When a customer orders 2 or more quantities of an item, packing teams frequently make the mistake of shipping only 1 unit. Our tool automatically stamps a prominent red "QTY: X (MULTI)" alert badge on the label top right to prevent under-shipment claims.

How does the SKU Dispatch Picklist help sellers?

Along with cropped labels, the tool generates a one-page SKU Summary & Picklist PDF showing total units needed per SKU code and courier breakdown, allowing your warehouse team to pick inventory before packing.

Why Raw Meesho A4 PDFs Cause Packing Delays and Pickup Rejections

When you download labels from the Meesho Supplier Panel (under Orders > Ready for Dispatch), Meesho bundles the shipping label (containing the tracking number, barcode, logistics provider like Valmo, Delhivery, Shadowfax, or Xpressbees, and recipient address) on the upper half of an A4 page, and the customer Tax Invoice on the lower half.

Attempting to print this uncropped A4 page directly onto 4×6 inch thermal roll printers causes the printer driver to scale down the full A4 canvas. This severely shrinks the barcode height and narrows the white quiet zones between bars. When delivery partners arrive for manifest pickup, their handheld barcode scanners fail to decode the tracking ID, causing delayed dispatch and penalties on seller scorecards.
