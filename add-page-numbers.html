Professional Offline PDF Pagination Service

Adding page numbers to PDF is a critical task for any official, academic, or professional document submission. Unlike cloud-based alternatives that pose serious privacy threats, The PDF Mechanic provides a browser-based local engine to paginate documents safely. We understand that your bank statements, academic research, and medical reports are confidential, and that’s why our "No Upload" infrastructure was designed using high-speed WebAssembly technology.

Technical Advantage & Document Safety

Most online PDF tools store your files on their storage for several hours, making your documents susceptible to indexing and leakage. The PDF Mechanic operates as an offline utility tool. Once this page is fully loaded in your mobile or PC browser, the application environment stays completely isolated. The numbering of PDF pages happens in your local RAM using the PDF-Lib core framework v1.17. Whether you need bates-numbering for legal filings or basic indices for report decks, the integrity of your document vectors is guaranteed without any resolution loss.

Comprehensive PDF Tools Integration

Our V2.4 rolling build supports deep integration with **Google Drive Read-Only Protocol** and **Dropbox API**. This unique hybrid logic allows you to pull PDF streams from global cloud clusters into our secure sandbox without exposing those files to any persistent network server. The mechanic logic serializes each page in memory, embeds high-fidelity Helvetica or custom font headers, and generates a fresh, clean output stream for instant download. No logs are recorded, and your session ID is cleared every time you click "Reset".

The Mechanic's FAQ & User Roadmap

Does it handle multi-column documents?

Yes, the position grid allows you to place page numbers in six unique zones, ensuring zero overlap with your original multi-column layouts and text artifacts.

Can I number large PDF Chapters separately?

Definitely. Our 'Initial Index' control lets you start numbering from any digit (e.g. start at 100), making it ideal for books or bundled documents spread across several PDF files.

Is my file ever seen by The PDF Mechanic staff?

Never. By design, we don't even possess a file-handling server. The tool lives as a static shell on our host and works solely through your browser's power.

Will numbering add watermarks or logos?

Absolutely not. Unlike commercial 'Freemium' tools, we do not inject any promo text or mechanical logos into your resulting documents. High privacy, no watermarks.
