Extracting tabular data from confidential financial files like **Bank Statements, Invoice ledgers, or Balance Sheets** requires maximum security. Traditional converters require uploading your raw files to remote cloud servers, which introduces a data privacy vulnerability.

The PDF Mechanic operates differently. Our client-side *PDF to Excel Converter* processes documents directly within your browser's runtime. Your files never leave your local environment. Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool work on Scanned PDFs or Images?

This specific parser reads "selectable text" layouts. If your PDF is scanned or is an image, you will need to run it through an OCR engine (Optical Character Recognition) to extract editable data.

Is there a strict page or file size limit?

Because processing runs natively in your hardware sandbox, memory limitations depend on your device's capacity. Generally, standard multi-page statements (up to 50 pages) process easily in a few seconds on most modern setups.

Is it completely free to extract tables to XLSX?

Yes, The PDF Mechanic's PDF to Excel converter is 100% free with no hidden charges, trial limits, or email registrations required.

What spreadsheet software is compatible with the converted files?

The downloaded file is a standard `.xlsx` spreadsheet. It is fully compatible with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers, and LibreOffice Calc.

Why are some rows misaligned after conversion?

Our parser clusters rows based on vertical alignment. If a PDF has complex, nested borders or multi-line cell text, certain rows may shift. For clean results, ensure the source document has standard table structures.
