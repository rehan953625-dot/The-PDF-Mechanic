Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support Amazon FBA Box Labels?

Yes! Upload your FBA carton/box label PDF and it will isolate each carton label for 4x6 roll sticker printing.

What printer settings should I use?

Set paper size to 4×6 inches (100×150 mm) and scale to 100% (Actual Size). Avoid "Fit to Page" to prevent barcode distortion.

High-Speed Amazon India Shipping Label Processing & Sorting

Amazon Seller Central exports combined Easy Ship documents with shipping barcodes on the top half and customer tax invoices on the bottom half. Direct printing forces scaling that degrades barcode scannability.

The PDF Mechanic Amazon Tool automatically detects Amazon markers (like Tax Invoice, Ordered Through Amazon, Tracking ID), crops the shipping label to standard 4x6" thermal size, sorts orders by SKU & Carrier (ATS / Delhivery / Blue Dart), and separates tax invoices into an independent PDF.
