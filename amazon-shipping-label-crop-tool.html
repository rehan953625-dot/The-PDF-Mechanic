Hardware Calibration Guide

Recommended Thermal Printer Settings for Amazon Easy Ship

To prevent barcode scanning failures during courier pickup and ensure crisp text on 203 DPI & 300 DPI thermal heads, configure your driver as follows:

Paper / Media Stock Size Set Page Size to 4.00 × 6.00 inches (or 100 × 150 mm). Never select Letter or A4 in printer preferences.
Print Scaling / Size Select Actual Size (100%). Disable "Fit to Printable Area" or "Shrink Oversized Pages" to maintain barcode bar ratios.
Darkness / Density Set darkness to 12 - 14 (on TSC TE244 / Zebra ZD230) to ensure deep black tracking bars without ink bleeding.
Print Speed Set print speed to 3 or 4 inches per second (ips) for the sharpest edge definition on 1D barcodes and QR codes.

The Problem with Direct A4 Printing on 4×6" Thermal Barcode Printers

Direct thermal printers such as the TSC TE244, Zebra ZD230, TVS LP 46 Neo, and Xprinter XP-420B are engineered to accept 4×6 inch (100×150 mm) roll stickers. When you send an uncropped A4 PDF directly to these printers, the print spooler attempts to scale down the entire A4 sheet. This results in two major operational problems:

  • Barcode Rasterization & Blur: Scaling down a full A4 sheet shrinks the 1D Code 128 tracking barcode below the minimum optical resolution (less than 10 mils), causing pickup scanners used by Amazon Transport Services (ATS), Delhivery, and Blue Dart to fail scanning.
  • Wasted Thermal Paper: Customer tax invoices are printed on expensive direct thermal sticker paper rather than standard laser paper, increasing your packaging supply costs.
