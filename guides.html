What You'll Find in These Guides

Every guide here starts from a specific, real problem rather than a general overview of what a PDF tool can do. "How do I get my scanned certificate under 200KB for an SSC application" is a different question from "how does PDF compression work," and it deserves a different kind of answer — one with the actual numbers, the actual portal quirks, and the actual steps, not just a definition. That's the standard every guide on this page is written to.

A few threads run through all of them. First, privacy: every tool these guides point to runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly, so nothing you upload — a bank statement, an Aadhaar card, a signed contract — ever reaches a server. Second, specificity: rather than one generic "how to use a PDF compressor" page, there's a dedicated guide for compressing to exactly 100KB for a government portal, a separate one for shrinking a report to fit Gmail's 25MB attachment limit, and another for resizing a passport photo to the exact pixel dimensions IBPS expects — because the right answer genuinely differs between those three cases.

The guides are grouped into four categories using the filter tabs above: general Tutorials on the core PDF operations (merging, splitting, converting, understanding the format itself), Security & Privacy deep-dives on why and how client-side processing works, Government Forms guides built specifically around Indian exam and banking portal requirements, and E-Commerce guides for marketplace sellers handling shipping labels at scale. New guides are added as new tools ship, usually covering whatever specific problem prompted the tool in the first place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all of The PDF Mechanic's guides free to read?

Yes. Every guide on this page is free, with no account, signup, or paywall of any kind — the same as every tool linked from them.

Do I need to create an account to use the tools linked in these guides?

No. None of the tools on The PDF Mechanic require an account, email address, or payment. Open the tool, use it, and download your result.

How often are new guides added?

New guides are added alongside new tools, typically every few weeks, usually covering a specific real-world problem — like government form photo sizing or offline Android usage — rather than a generic overview.

Do these guides apply outside India?

Most of the underlying PDF and image techniques (merging, splitting, compressing, converting) apply anywhere. A few guides — like government job form sizing and e-commerce marketplace label cropping — are written specifically around Indian portals and marketplaces (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, Railway RRB, Meesho, Flipkart, Amazon Easy Ship) since those have their own specific requirements.
